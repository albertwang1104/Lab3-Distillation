\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We implemented SDS, VSD, and SDI to optimize latent images from text prompts using a frozen diffusion prior. Across ten prompts, their mean CLIP scores are 0.3004, 0.2825, and 0.3045, respectively. SDI gives the best average alignment and often the clearest structure, while SDS leads on more individual prompts. VSD produces recognizable subjects but also persistent fragmented texture and the lowest mean score. Together, the numerical and visual results show that the choice of guidance reference affects both prompt alignment and image appearance; the small SDS--SDI score difference also calls for care when generalizing beyond these runs.
